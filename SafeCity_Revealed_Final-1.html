04 Field notes

Where judgment matters.

What does Safe City really do?

In Lahore, PSCA describes a traffic system that combines red-light monitoring, journey-time monitoring and electronic notices. Its broader command centre also supports emergency response and incident management. PSCA traffic management ↗ · About PSCA ↗

How close is this model to the real thing?

PSCA describes trained models that use both single images and movement across frames. Here you can inspect a colour mask, a fixed road scale and a tiny alphabet of pixel templates. Fine amounts, time gaps, weight limits and other thresholds in this exhibition are illustrative. PSCA’s system description ↗

When should a person review the evidence?

A hidden target can shift the measured centre. A missing frame can conceal the crossing. Dirt can make one character look like another. A reviewer needs the sequence, the rule and the original images to judge an uncertain result. An unreadable plate withholds the demo notice.
